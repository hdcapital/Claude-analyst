## Thesis
Auric Mining has moved from trading-halt speculation to a concrete, binding acquisition of the Union Jack Project, adding 102koz Inferred gold resource (51% uplift to 301koz company-wide) at a modest initial consideration of A$9.6m cash/shares plus milestone payments and a 1% royalty. The deal is strategically logical, sitting 15km from Auric's Burbanks mill which management aims to re-establish as a processing hub by 2028, and offers near-term open-pit optionality with high-grade intercepts supporting resource growth. Key watch items: completion of due diligence on the M15/120 lease, dilution from share issuance and milestone payments, and conversion of Inferred resource to higher-confidence categories via the planned A$2m five-year drilling program.
